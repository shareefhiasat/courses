# Academic Performance

This guide outlines the academic performance standards, warning procedures, and dismissal policies for students in the diploma program.

## Academic Warning System

### First Academic Warning

A student receives an academic warning if their semester GPA falls below the required threshold. This serves as an early indicator of academic difficulty.

### Final Academic Warning

A student receives a **final academic warning** if they:

- Repeatedly obtain a semester GPA of less than 1.5 in the second semester
- Continue to show poor academic performance despite initial warnings

### Academic Coordinator Follow-up

The academic coordinator is assigned to follow up with students who have:

- A cumulative GPA of less than 1.5
- Received academic warnings

Each student in this category receives:

- An **individual academic support plan**
- Academic guidance and counseling
- Improvement options and recommendations
- Regular monitoring of progress

## Academic Dismissal

### Grounds for Dismissal

A student may be dismissed from the diploma program if any of the following conditions are met:

#### 1. Consecutive Academic Warnings

- The student receives **three consecutive academic warnings** without showing improvement
- Each warning is issued based on poor semester GPA performance
- No significant academic progress is demonstrated between warnings

#### 2. Lack of Commitment

- The student's **lack of seriousness** is proven
- The student fails to commit to the **approved academic plan**
- Evidence of neglecting academic responsibilities is documented

#### 3. Exceeding Study Duration

- The student **exceeds the maximum period allowed for study**
- The program has specific time limits for completion
- Extensions are not granted or have been exhausted

### Dismissal Process

When continued poor performance is observed after a final warning:

1. **Report Submission**: The academic coordinator submits a detailed report to the Academic Affairs Council
2. **Council Review**: The Academic Affairs Council reviews the student's situation
3. **Decision Making**: The Council takes necessary action according to approved policies
4. **Implementation**: Academic dismissal may be implemented if the situation warrants it

## Academic Support Services

### Individual Academic Support Plan

Students with academic difficulties receive personalized support:

- **Academic Guidance**: One-on-one counseling with the academic coordinator
- **Study Skills**: Training in effective study techniques
- **Time Management**: Assistance with organizing academic workload
- **Resource Referral**: Connection to tutoring and additional learning resources
- **Progress Monitoring**: Regular check-ins to track improvement

### Improvement Options

The academic coordinator may recommend:

- Reduced course load for the next semester
- Specific tutoring in weak subject areas
- Attendance at academic workshops
- Participation in study groups
- Additional practice assignments

## GPA Requirements

### Minimum GPA Standards

Students must maintain:

- **Semester GPA**: Minimum of 1.5 to avoid academic warnings
- **Cumulative GPA**: Minimum of 1.5 to remain in good standing
- **Graduation GPA**: As per the cumulative GPA classification table

### GPA Monitoring

- GPA is calculated at the end of each semester
- Students are notified if their GPA falls below standards
- Academic warnings are issued when thresholds are not met
- Continued low GPA triggers dismissal procedures

## Resubmission and Supplementary Exams

### Course Resubmission

Students may resubmit coursework according to program policies:

- Specific conditions apply for resubmission eligibility
- Resubmission grading follows the course retake scale
- Maximum grade for retaken courses is B+ (3.5 points)

### Supplementary Exams

- Supplementary exams may be offered under certain conditions
- Academic performance affects eligibility for supplementary exams
- Specific rules govern supplementary exam grading

## School of Signals and Information Technology Rights

The School of Signals and Information Technology reserves the right to:

- Update or amend these regulations
- Make changes that serve the academic interest of students
- Adjust requirements to meet educational quality standards
- Implement new policies as needed

## Appeals Process

Students facing academic dismissal may:

1. Submit a written appeal to the Academic Affairs Council
2. Provide documentation of extenuating circumstances
3. Request a review of their academic record
4. Present evidence of improvement potential

The Council reviews appeals on a case-by-case basis.
