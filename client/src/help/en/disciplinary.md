# Disciplinary Actions

This guide outlines the behaviors that constitute violations requiring student punishment, and the procedures for handling disciplinary cases.

## Violations

The following behaviors expose students to disciplinary actions according to school regulations:

### 1. Cheating

- **Proven cheating**: When an act of cheating is proven with material evidence
- **Attempted cheating**: When a student attempts to cheat, even if unsuccessful
- **Assisting in cheating**: Helping another student to cheat or facilitating cheating

### 2. Impersonation

- **Identity fraud**: Pretending to be another student during exams or assessments
- **Proxy attendance**: Having someone else attend class or take exams on your behalf

### 3. Exam Rule Violations

- **Unauthorized materials**: Bringing prohibited materials into exam halls
- **Communication**: Attempting to communicate with other students during exams
- **Leaving exam area**: Leaving the exam area without permission
- **Early departure**: Leaving the exam before the designated end time without authorization

### 4. Forgery

- **Document forgery**: Forgery in school documents or records
- **Using forged papers**: Using forged documents for school purposes
- **False information**: Providing false information in official documents

### 5. Public Order Violations

- **Disruptive behavior**: Any behavior that disrupts public order
- **Based on recommendation**: Violations identified by the head teacher and approved by the school principal

### 6. Other Violations

- **Any other violations**: Any other actions that constitute violations as determined by school leadership

## Disciplinary Procedures

### During an Exam

When a cheating incident is detected during an exam:

1. **Exam Withdrawal**: The exam paper is immediately withdrawn from the student
2. **Incident Report**: The supervisor who caught the cheating incident submits a detailed report with supporting evidence to the department
3. **Council Referral**: The department refers the report and its attachments to the Academic Affairs Council
4. **Decision Making**: The Academic Affairs Council makes an appropriate decision based on the incident, according to the diploma study system regulations
5. **Implementation**: The decision is forwarded to the head teachers for review and to take any other actions stipulated by the regulations

### After an Exam (During Correction)

When cheating is suspected during exam correction:

1. **Suspicion Report**: The teacher who suspects cheating submits a detailed report with supporting evidence to the Academic Affairs wing
2. **Wing Referral**: The Academic Affairs wing refers the report and its attachments to the head teachers
3. **Review Process**: The head teachers review the case to make an appropriate decision regarding verification
4. **Verification**: The case is verified and appropriate actions are taken

## Disciplinary Punishments

### Types of Punishments

In case of violating academic and disciplinary commitments, the following penalties can be applied:

#### a. Dismissal from Study

- **Dismissal from the diploma program** when any of the dismissal reasons are fulfilled
- Permanent removal from the academic program
- No possibility of readmission under the same circumstances

#### b. Grade Deduction

- **Deduction of grades** from courses or assessments
- Reduction in GPA or specific course grades
- Impact on academic standing

#### c. Warning

- **Warning** issued to the student
- Formal notification of unacceptable behavior
- Documented in student record

#### d. Admonition

- **Admonition** (stronger than warning)
- Formal reprimand for serious violations
- May include required counseling

#### e. Salary Deduction

- **Salary deduction** not exceeding 1,000 Qatari Riyals
- According to the general regulations of the Ministry of Defense, Article (160) and Article (156)
- Applies to sponsored students or employees

#### f. Field Penalties

- **Field penalties** as per military regulations
- Physical training or corrective measures
- Supervised by military personnel

#### g. Detention

- **Closed/open detention** according to the general regulations of the Ministry of Defense
- Article (160) and Article (156) of the regulations
- Duration based on severity of violation

## Prohibited Behaviors

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

### 1. Verbal Abuse

- **Abusive language**: Using offensive language that offends honor and dignity
- **Targets**: Faculty, administrators, supervisors, or colleagues
- **Zero tolerance**: No tolerance for disrespectful language

### 2. Attendance Negligence

- **Negligence in timing**: Being negligent in attending classes at specified times
- **Locations**: Lecture halls or practical training laboratories
- **Repeated offenses**: Chronic lateness or absence

### 3. Smoking Violations

- **Smoking in prohibited areas**: Smoking inside study halls
- **During training**: Smoking during practical training sessions
- **Designated areas only**: Smoking only in designated smoking areas

### 4. Unauthorized Presence

- **Leadership offices**: Being present in front of the offices of the School of Signals and Information Technology leadership
- **Exception**: Only when summoned or with explicit permission

### 5. Cheating

- **Exam cheating**: Any form of cheating in examinations
- **Academic dishonesty**: All forms of academic dishonesty
- **Zero tolerance**: Strict prohibition

### 6. Unauthorized Movement

- **Visitor areas**: Moving to visitor areas in private cars
- **Unless instructed**: Only when there are specific instructions to do so
- **Security protocol**: Must follow security procedures

### 7. Unlisted Violations

- **Scope of prohibitions**: Anything not mentioned but falling within the scope of prohibitions
- **Leadership discretion**: Subject to the discretion and decision of the School of Signals and Information Technology leadership

## Academic Advising

### Importance of Academic Advising

Academic advising plays a significant role in:

- **Integration**: Helping students integrate into the academic environment
- **Adaptation**: Assisting students to adapt academically, psychologically, and socially to the credit hour system
- **Problem prevention**: Making academic advising clear to students to prevent problems that hinder their academic progress
- **Expert guidance**: Relying on experienced and competent individuals

### Advisor Commitment

- The academy is committed to making academic advising clear and accessible
- Many students have achieved better educational levels through commitment to their academic advisors
- Advisors provide encouragement and motivation for excellence
- Regular meetings with advisors are encouraged
