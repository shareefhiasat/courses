# Grading & GPA

This guide explains the grading system, GPA calculation methods, and academic performance standards used throughout the program.

## Grading Scale

The following grading scale applies to all courses:

| Symbol | Description | Percentage Range | Grade Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Excellent | 100 - 90% | 4.0 |
| **B+** | Very Good High | 85 - less than 90% | 3.5 |
| **B** | Very Good | 80 - less than 85% | 3.0 |
| **C+** | Good High | 75 - less than 80% | 2.5 |
| **C** | Good | 70 - less than 75% | 2.0 |
| **D+** | Acceptable High | 65 - less than 70% | 1.5 |
| **D** | Acceptable | 60 - less than 65% | 1.0 |
| **F** | Fail | Less than 60% | 0.0 |
| **WF** | Mandatory Withdrawal | - | - |
| **FA** | Fail - Absence from Final Exam | - | - |
| **FB** | Fail - Exceeding Absence Limit (20%) | - | - |

### Special Grade Codes

- **WF (Mandatory Withdrawal)**: Student was required to withdraw from the course
- **FA (Fail - Absence)**: Student failed due to absence from the final exam without providing an acceptable excuse
- **FB (Fail - Absence Limit)**: Student failed due to exceeding the permitted absence rate of 20%

## Course Retake Grading

When a student retakes a course, the following grading scale applies:

| Symbol | Description | Percentage Range | Grade Points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **B+** | Very Good High | 100 - 85% | 3.5 |
| **B** | Very Good | 80 - less than 85% | 3.0 |
| **C+** | Good High | 75 - less than 80% | 2.5 |
| **C** | Good | 70 - less than 75% | 2.0 |
| **D+** | Acceptable High | 65 - less than 70% | 1.5 |
| **D** | Acceptable | 60 - less than 65% | 1.0 |
| **F** | Fail | Less than 60% | 0.0 |
| **WF** | Mandatory Withdrawal | - | - |
| **FA** | Fail - Absence from Final Exam | - | - |
| **FB** | Fail - Exceeding Absence Limit (20%) | - | - |

**Note**: When retaking a course, the maximum achievable grade is B+ (3.5 points), and the highest percentage range is 100-85%.

## GPA Calculation

### Semester GPA

The Semester GPA is calculated as:

```
Semester GPA = Sum of points earned in the relevant semester
                ────────────────────────────────────────────────
                Sum of registered hours in the relevant semester
```

### Cumulative GPA

The Cumulative GPA is calculated as:

```
Cumulative GPA = Total sum of earned points
                 ──────────────────────────────
                 Total sum of registered hours
```

## Cumulative GPA Classification

Upon graduation, the student's general grade is classified as follows:

| Symbol | Description | GPA Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A** | Excellent | 4.0 - 3.6 |
| **B** | Very Good | 2.8 - less than 3.6 |
| **C** | Good | 2.0 - less than 2.8 |
| **D** | Acceptable | 1.5 - less than 2.0 |
| **F** | Weak | Less than 1.5 |

## Academic Performance Standards

### Academic Warnings

- A student receives a **final academic warning** if they repeatedly obtain a semester GPA of less than 1.5 in the second semester
- The academic coordinator follows up with students who have a cumulative GPA of less than 1.5
- An individual academic support plan is prepared for each such student, including academic guidance and improvement options

### Academic Dismissal

A student may be dismissed from the diploma program if:

- They receive three consecutive academic warnings without improvement
- Their lack of seriousness or commitment to the approved academic plan is proven
- They exceed the maximum period allowed for study

### Continued Poor Performance

If a student continues to perform poorly after receiving a final warning:

- A report is submitted by the academic coordinator to the Academic Affairs Council
- The Council considers the student's situation and takes necessary action according to approved policies
- This may include academic dismissal if the situation warrants it

## Academic Excellence

### Honors Recognition

A student may graduate **with honors** if they meet the following criteria:

- Achieve a cumulative GPA of 3.6 or higher in the graduation semester
- Receive a "Very Good" or higher grade throughout the entire academic study period
- Have not received a written warning or military trial for a serious violation according to internal regulations

### Certificate of Appreciation

A student may receive a **Certificate of Appreciation** if they:

- Achieve a semester GPA of 4.00 for the current spring semester or the past fall semester
- Maintain a general GPA of 4.00 until the current spring semester
- Have the best graduation project in the academic year

## Attendance Requirements

### Exam Eligibility

A student is **prohibited from entering the final exam** in any course if:

- Their absence rate exceeds 20% of the total actual hours for the course
- This applies whether the absence is with or without an excuse

### Absence Policies

- The permitted absence rate is 20% of total course hours
- Exceeding this limit results in automatic failure (FB grade)
- Excused absences still count toward the 20% limit
- Students must provide acceptable documentation for excused absences
