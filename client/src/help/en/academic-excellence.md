# Academic Excellence

This guide outlines the recognition programs for outstanding academic achievement and the academic advising system available to all students.

## Academic Excellence Recognition

### Graduation with Honors

Students may graduate **with honors** if they meet the following criteria:

#### Requirements

- **Cumulative GPA**: Achieve a cumulative GPA of 3.6 or higher in the graduation semester
- **Consistent Performance**: Receive a "Very Good" or higher grade throughout the entire academic study period
- **Clean Record**: Have not received a written warning or military trial for a serious violation according to internal regulations

#### Benefits

- **Honors Distinction**: Special recognition on graduation certificate and transcripts
- **Ceremonial Recognition**: Honors designation during graduation ceremony
- **Academic Record**: Permanent notation of honors achievement in academic record

### Certificate of Appreciation

Students may receive a **Certificate of Appreciation** for exceptional academic performance.

#### Semester-Based Recognition

**For Current Semester:**
- Achieve a semester GPA of 4.00 for the current spring semester

**For Previous Semester:**
- Achieve a semester GPA of 4.00 for the past fall semester

#### Cumulative Recognition

- Maintain a general GPA of 4.00 until the current spring semester
- Demonstrates consistent excellence across multiple semesters

#### Project-Based Recognition

- Have the best graduation project in the academic year
- Evaluated by faculty committee based on innovation, quality, and relevance

#### Benefits

- **Formal Certificate**: Official certificate of appreciation from the school
- **Public Recognition**: Announcement in school communications
- **Portfolio Enhancement**: Valuable addition to academic and professional portfolio

## Academic Advising

### Importance of Academic Advising

Academic advising is a critical component of student success and plays a significant role in:

#### Integration into Academic Environment

- **Orientation**: Helping students understand the academic system and requirements
- **Cultural Adaptation**: Assisting students in adapting to the academic culture
- **Resource Connection**: Connecting students with available academic resources

#### Adaptation to Credit Hour System

- **Academic Adaptation**: Helping students adapt academically to the credit hour system
- **Psychological Adaptation**: Supporting students' psychological adjustment to the academic workload
- **Social Adaptation**: Facilitating social integration within the academic community

#### Problem Prevention

- **Proactive Guidance**: Making academic advising clear to prevent problems before they arise
- **Early Intervention**: Identifying and addressing issues that could hinder academic progress
- **Strategic Planning**: Helping students plan their academic path effectively

### Advisor Qualifications

The academy is committed to providing high-quality academic advising by:

- **Experienced Advisors**: Relying on individuals with extensive academic experience
- **Competent Guidance**: Ensuring advisors are knowledgeable about program requirements
- **Professional Development**: Continuous training for advisors on best practices

### Student Success Through Advising

Many students have achieved better educational levels through:

- **Commitment to Advisors**: Regularly meeting with and following advisor guidance
- **Active Engagement**: Actively participating in the advising process
- **Implementation**: Applying advisor recommendations to their academic approach

### Advisor Encouragement and Motivation

Academic advisors provide:

- **Encouragement**: Motivating students to strive for excellence
- **Goal Setting**: Helping students set realistic and challenging academic goals
- **Progress Monitoring**: Tracking student progress and celebrating achievements
- **Support**: Providing support during challenging academic periods

### Making the Most of Academic Advising

#### Best Practices for Students

- **Regular Meetings**: Schedule regular meetings with your academic advisor
- **Preparation**: Come prepared with questions and academic progress updates
- **Open Communication**: Be honest about challenges and concerns
- **Follow-up**: Implement advisor recommendations and report back on results

#### When to Seek Advising

- **Course Selection**: Before selecting courses for the upcoming semester
- **Academic Difficulties**: When experiencing academic challenges
- **Goal Changes**: When considering changes to academic or career goals
- **Program Changes**: When considering changing programs or specializations
